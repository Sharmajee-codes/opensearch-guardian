# OpenSearch Guardian Governance

## Maintainers

OpenSearch Guardian is maintained by a group of volunteers who have demonstrated sustained contributions to the project. Maintainers have merge rights and help steer the project's direction.

Current maintainers can be found in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/opensearch-guardian/opensearch-guardian/graphs/contributors).

Becoming a maintainer is based on meritocratic contributions including code, documentation, issue triage, and community support.

## Decision Process

Technical decisions are made through Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) stored in `docs/adr/`.

- Proposals for significant architectural changes should be submitted as ADR pull requests
- ADRs follow the lightweight template in `docs/adr/template.md`
- Decisions are made by maintainer consensus after community discussion
- Once accepted, ADRs are immutable records of past decisions
- Minor changes and bug fixes follow the standard contribution process without requiring an ADR

## Roadmap Transparency

The project maintains a public roadmap to provide transparency about planned features and releases.

- Roadmap is documented in `docs/operations/roadmap.md`
- Release versions and scope are defined in the SOP section 11 (Delivery Roadmap)
- The roadmap is updated regularly based on community feedback and maintainer priorities
- Long-term vision is outlined in SOP section 12 (Future Scope)
- Acceptance criteria for milestones are defined in SOP section 13

## Community Governance

OpenSearch Guardian follows an open governance model:

- All development happens in the open on GitHub
- Issues and discussions are public and accessible to everyone
- Release planning occurs in public milestones
- The project strives for consensus-based decision making
- Maintainers act as stewards of the project's long-term health and sustainability

## Changes to Governance

This governance document may be updated over time as the project evolves. Significant changes to governance processes will be discussed openly in the community before implementation.
